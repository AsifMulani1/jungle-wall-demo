On the wall now

    1. Each model is slimmed for the wall: the elephant went from 1.5 million to 30,000 triangles.
    2. The colouring sheet is traced from the model's exact side view. The rhino's legs and tail are outlined using its own skeleton.
    3. The drawing is projected onto both sides. Hidden areas borrow the colour of the nearest visible surface.
    4. The elephant's skeleton was built automatically from its body map, and its ten animations were keyframed in code. The rhino uses its own skeleton and walk cycle, plus added moves.
    5. An acting layer makes them look at taps, hop, trumpet, snort and greet each other.

    These are free test models in two different styles: a cartoon elephant and a realistic rhino. The firm's own animals, made in one consistent style, go through the same steps.

    Elephant: "Cute Stylized Elephant" by ND Animation Studio. Rhino: "Animated Rhinoceros Walking Animal Loop" by LasquetiSpice. Plants: "Jungle Environment" by Bindestrek. All from Sketchfab under CC Attribution.
